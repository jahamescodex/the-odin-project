Taco Recipe

Home

Description

This is a fun, portable dish right inside a baf of chips. Reviewers report that their kids love eating this for dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beaf
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1 (1 ounce) pacakge taco seasong mix
  • 4 (2.5 ounce) packages corn chips
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup shtredded Cheddar cheese
  • 1 chopped fresh tomatoe
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/3 cup salsa

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Cook and stir ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat until browned and crumbly, 7 to 10 minutes. Drain excess oil. Mix in water and taco seasoning. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 5 minutes, stirring ocassionally.
  3. Gently crush corn chips in the sealed bags. Snip one top and one bottom corner off each bag, then cut open along the side edge.
  4. Spoon equal amounts of beef, lettuce, Cheddar, tomato, sour cream, and salsa into each back on top of crushed chips.
  5. Serve in the bag and eat using a fork. Enjoy!
